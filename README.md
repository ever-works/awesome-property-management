## Tenant & Landlord Apps

- [Avail](https://www.avail.co/) - Landlord software for rental listings, tenant screening, and rent collection. It is designed for independent landlords. A freemium plan is available. ([Read more](/details/avail.md)) `Freemium`
- [Innago](https://www.innago.com/) - Free property management software for landlords and tenants. It offers rent collection, leasing, and maintenance tracking. A freemium model exists for advanced features. ([Read more](/details/innago.md)) `Freemium`
- [RentRedi](https://www.rentredi.com/) - Landlord and tenant app for managing rental properties. It includes rent collection, tenant screening, and maintenance requests. Pricing is paid per unit. ([Read more](/details/rentredi.md)) `Paid` `API`
- [Stessa](https://www.stessa.com/) - Free financial dashboard for real estate investors. It tracks income, expenses, and property performance. It is free to use. ([Read more](/details/stessa.md)) `Free Plan` `Free`
- [TenantCloud](https://www.tenantcloud.com/) - Free and paid property management software for landlords and tenants. It supports rent payments, leasing, and accounting. A freemium model is available. ([Read more](/details/tenantcloud.md)) `Freemium` `API`
- [Zillow Rental Manager](https://www.zillow.com/rental-manager) - Tool for listing rentals and screening tenants on Zillow. It allows landlords to collect applications and reports. It offers free and paid options. ([Read more](/details/zillow-rental-manager.md)) `Freemium`
